Villanelle Rails Playground

An editor with Rails highlighting. Load one of the six programs, then change glyphs: edit a role to swap it everywhere, or click a palette glyph to insert it at the cursor. Your text and roles are remembered in this browser.

Glyph roles

        
Change a glyph and every occurrence in the editor changes with it, along with the highlighting. Click a field, then a palette glyph, to assign it.

        

        

      

      

        
Palette

        
Every glyph here exists in the embedded font at cell width. Click to insert at the cursor.
